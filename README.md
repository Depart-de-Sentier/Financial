# Départ de Sentier – Financial Transparency

Départ de Sentier (DdS) is committed to transparency and responsible financial governance.  

This repository provides publicly accessible financial information related to the association’s activities, including conferences, schools, and projects.

Historically, this repository also included detailed financial documents such as monthly bank statements and transaction-level expense records. Following a review of data-protection and governance considerations, this practice has been revised.

## Update (24.12.2025)

To protect personal data and comply with Swiss and European data-protection standards, DdS has discontinued the publication of raw bank statements and transaction-level financial records.

Instead, DdS publishes:
- Annual financial summary reports (PDF), including the income statement, balance sheet, and explanatory notes, providing an aggregated overview of the association’s finances.

These documents ensure a transparent and accurate view of DdS’s financial position and activities without exposing sensitive personal or transactional data.

Detailed financial records (including bank statements) are retained internally and are available to the competent authorities upon request.
