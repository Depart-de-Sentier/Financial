# Départ de Sentier – Note for Financial Summaries 2022–2024

The financial summaries for the years 2022–2024 were uploaded at the end of 2025.  
The values may contain **very minor deviations** from the versions approved by the respective General Assemblies.

These deviations result exclusively from:
- improved foreign exchange rate treatments, consistently applied over the full financial year (from 1 January to 31 December),
- adaptations to Swiss accounting rules and accepted accounting practice,
- minor technical or presentation-related adjustments,
- or the correction of immaterial clerical errors.

The adjustments are **non-material**, do **not affect the overall financial position**, and do **not alter the substance of the results or the non-profit purpose** of the association. The General Assemblies approved the annual accounts **in substance**, and the published summaries reflect the most accurate and consistent versions for transparency and documentation purposes.
